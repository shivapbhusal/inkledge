I like: 

Leisure. Long walk among the woods. Slow conversation. Warmth. Poetry. Books. Melancholy. An old man in a kimono. Šaltibarščiai. A bowl of rice. Kefir. Kabir. Bhupi Sherchan. Devkota. Flaubert. Floor desk. Forest. Forgiveness. Salt lamp. Chekhov. Cricket. Pickleball. Sunlight. Darkness. Rain. A free T-shirt from a conference. Love, even when it's not reciprocal. Grandparents. Elderly parents. Sitting beside someone who is in the late stage of their life. Time with family, friends— for the widest possible meaning of the two words. Writing beautiful code. Python community. Open Source. Linus Torvalds, Richard Stallman. Tibetan culture. Singing bowl. Small creatures. Small houses. Someone from a small country. Public bus. Haiku. Haibun. Basho. Issa.

Late-night parties. Scorching heat. Social Media. Short videos. Attention economy. Designer clothes. Large houses. Late-stage capitalism. Formal clothes (especially when it’s mandated to wear them). Sofa. Television on a loudspeaker. Authoritarianism. Fascism. War. Violence and its silent forms. Cultures that dominate media. Cultures that tower over other cultures. Advertisement. Gossip magazines. Pickup trucks. Imperialism. Certification exams. Unwalkable cities.
